The RNG gods finally caught up with me, and after working as a software developer for
21 years, it was my turn to get laid off.

It happened around the end of May which meant that I had a few months to enjoy
the nice warm weather, get some long overdue house projects done and get a chance
to de-stress.

One thing I misjudged was how much "free" time I would actually have to catch up
on video games, shows and books. That turned out to be very little in reality.

Still, I managed to finish a few books and found a new fantasy series that I really
like (the Death Gate Cycle).

As a fun and light warmup, I decided to migrate my old blog to Hugo and also
to write a little scraper for the local canal bridges in the area (more on that in
a separate blog entry).

Anyways, I'm ready to be back doing what I like and hoping to get a fun and challenging
position working as an Elixir developer again.
